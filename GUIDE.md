# Vault 101: Brainrot Rebirth

A text-based RPG game set in a post-apocalyptic world. Choose your character, battle enemies, collect loot, and survive in the wasteland!

## 🎮 Game Features

- **Three Unique Characters**: Choose from TralaleroTralala, TungTungTungSahur, or CappuccinoAssassino
- **Combat System**: Turn-based combat with strategic elements
- **Enemy Types**: Face various enemies including Crawlers, Brutes, Screechers, and Overgrown Brutes
- **Loot System**: Collect and manage items throughout your journey
- **Character Progression**: Gain experience and level up your character

## 🎯 How to Play

### Getting Started

1. **Launch the Game**: Run the game using the instructions in README.md
2. **Character Selection**: When the game starts, you'll be presented with three character options:

   - **TralaleroTralala**: Balanced fighter with well-rounded stats
   - **TungTungTungSahur**: Specialized combat character
   - **CappuccinoAssassino**: Unique playstyle with special abilities

3. **Choose Your Character**: Enter the number corresponding to your preferred character

### Game Navigation

- **Map System**: Navigate through different locations in the wasteland
- **Explore Areas**: Each location may contain enemies, loot, or story events
- **Move Between Locations**: Use the map interface to travel to different areas

### Combat System

**Turn-Based Battles**:

- Combat is turn-based, alternating between you and the enemy
- Choose your actions carefully to maximize damage and minimize damage taken

**Combat Actions**:

- **Attack**: Deal damage to the enemy
- **Defend**: Reduce incoming damage
- **Use Items**: Consume items from your inventory (if available)
- **Special Abilities**: Some characters have unique combat abilities

**Combat Tips**:

- Pay attention to enemy types - each has different strengths and weaknesses
- Monitor your health and use defensive actions when needed
- Plan your attacks strategically to defeat stronger enemies

### Enemy Types

- **Crawler**: Fast but weak ground units
- **Brute**: Strong melee fighters with high health
- **Screecher**: Flying units that can evade ground attacks
- **Overgrown Brute**: Enhanced version of Brutes with devastating power

### Inventory & Loot System

- **Collecting Loot**: Defeat enemies and explore locations to find items
- **Managing Inventory**: View and organize your collected items
- **Using Items**: Items can be used in and out of combat for various effects

### Character Progression

- **Gaining Experience**: Earn XP by defeating enemies
- **Leveling Up**: Accumulate enough XP to increase your character level
- **Stat Improvements**: Each level increases your character's stats (health, attack, defense)
- **Growing Stronger**: Higher levels allow you to face tougher enemies

### Game Objectives

- Survive encounters with increasingly difficult enemies
- Explore all areas of the wasteland
- Collect powerful loot and equipment
- Reach the highest level possible
- Master the combat system and defeat the toughest foes

### Tips for Success

1. **Start Slow**: Begin by fighting weaker enemies to gain levels
2. **Explore Thoroughly**: Check all locations for valuable loot
3. **Manage Resources**: Don't waste items on easy fights
4. **Learn Enemy Patterns**: Each enemy type has predictable behaviors
5. **Strategic Defense**: Sometimes defending is better than attacking
6. **Level Grinding**: If stuck, fight easier enemies to gain levels

### Controls & Interface

- Follow the on-screen prompts and menus
- Enter numbers or letters as indicated to make choices
- Read combat messages carefully for important information
- Pay attention to your current stats displayed during gameplay
